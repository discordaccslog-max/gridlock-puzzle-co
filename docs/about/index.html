Gridlock Puzzle Co.

About

Gridlock Puzzle Co. makes puzzle books for people who like the back page of the newspaper best.

We publish collections of sudoku, crosswords, cryptograms, logic grids and word searches. Each collection is an online puzzle book: you buy it once, unlock it with a key, and play it in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Every puzzle can also be printed, because some puzzles are simply better with a pencil.

How the puzzles are made

We write our own word lists, clues, sayings and stories, and we build every grid with software we wrote for the purpose. Before a puzzle goes into a book, a solver checks it. Every sudoku has exactly one solution under its rules, and its difficulty is set by the hardest solving technique it needs. Every logic grid has one solution and can be finished by deduction alone. Every cryptogram has exactly one readable answer. In every word search, each word appears exactly once. Any puzzle that fails a check is thrown out and replaced.

Why we do it this way

We wanted puzzle books that are fair: no guessing, no broken grids, and no clues that only make sense to the person who wrote them. We also wanted them to be easy to own. There is no account to create, nothing to install, and nothing tracking how you play. Your key opens your books, and your progress stays on your own device.

Two shelves

The Core Books cover the five classic puzzle types, each from easy to expert. The Expert Editions go further with added rules, larger grids and fewer starting clues, and every Expert Edition page says exactly what is different.
