Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Digital products only

All Gridlock Puzzle Co. products are digital puzzle books played online. There is no physical shipment.

Instant delivery

After you pay, your license key appears immediately on the order confirmation page. Please copy your key and save it somewhere safe; the page has buttons to copy it and to save it as a text file.

Opening your puzzles

Enter your key on the site’s Unlock page and your puzzles open right away. The same key works on any of your devices: a computer, tablet or phone with a modern web browser.

Lost keys

If you lose your key, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we will send you a replacement.

How long you have access

Online access continues for as long as Gridlock Puzzle Co. operates. Every puzzle can also be printed, along with its answer key, so you can keep a paper copy.
