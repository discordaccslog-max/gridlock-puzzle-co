Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What you receive

Every Gridlock Puzzle Co. product is digital: one or more online puzzle books that you play in a web browser. When you buy, you receive a license key that unlocks the book or books in your purchase. There are no files to download and nothing to install.

How delivery works, step by step

  1. Agree and check out. On the product page, tick the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Refund Policy, then select Buy now. You pay on our payment provider’s secure checkout page.
  2. Payment goes through. As soon as your payment is complete, the payment provider sends you back to our order confirmation page for your product.
  3. Your key is delivered. The order confirmation page shows your license key straight away, together with the product name, the date and time, and your order reference when the payment provider supplies one. Delivery is complete at this point.
  4. Keep a copy. Use Copy key, Save confirmation (a PDF of your order details and key) or Print. We do not send the key by email, so please save it before you leave the page.
  5. Unlock. Select Open my puzzles, or enter your key on the Unlock page. Your books appear on your library shelf right away.
  6. Play. Open a book and play in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Every puzzle can also be printed.

When delivery happens

Immediately after your payment is complete, on the order confirmation page. There is no waiting period and no manual processing.

No physical shipment

Nothing is mailed or shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

Using more than one device

The same key works on any of your own devices. On each new device, enter the key on the Unlock page. Your puzzle progress is saved separately on each device.

Lost keys

If you lose your key, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we will send you a replacement.

How long you have access

Online access continues for as long as Gridlock Puzzle Co. operates. Every puzzle can also be printed, along with its answer key, so you can keep a paper copy.
