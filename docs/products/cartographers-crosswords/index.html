How the online player works

  • Play in your browser: tap or click a square, then type, or use the on-screen keypad or letter keyboard so your phone’s keyboard never covers the grid.
  • Full keyboard support with arrow keys, Tab and Backspace.
  • Check a single entry or the whole puzzle, take a hint, or reveal the solution.
  • An optional timer you can hide, and pencil marks for sudoku.
  • Progress saves automatically on your device, with a reset button on every puzzle.
  • Print any puzzle on a clean page, and print its answer key separately.
  • Filter your library by type, variant, difficulty and solved status.

What you need

A modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Puzzles load from the web, so you need a connection to open them; your progress is stored in that browser.
