Refund Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Full refunds within 14 days

You can have a full refund on any purchase if you ask within 14 days of buying it. You do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

To request a refund, contact us through the Contact page and include your order number. Including the email address you used at checkout helps us find your order quickly.

When you will get your money back

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We issue them within 5 business days of your request. Your payment provider may take a few more days to show the refund on your statement.
