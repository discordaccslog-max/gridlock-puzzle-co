Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Gridlock Puzzle Co. handles and why. The short version: we collect as little as we can, and we never sell it.

No cookies, analytics or trackers

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Your keys and progress stay on your device

License keys you enter and your puzzle progress are saved only in your own browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can remove your keys at any time with Sign out on this device in your library, and clear a puzzle’s progress with Reset progress. Clearing your browser’s site data removes both.

Payments

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider, which collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. Gridlock Puzzle Co. never sees your full card details.

Order information we receive

From the payment provider we receive order information: your name, your email address, the products you bought and your country. We use it only to provide support, to process refunds, and to meet our tax and accounting obligations.

We never sell your information

Your personal information is never sold, and never shared for advertising.

Hosting and fonts

Like any website, the pages are delivered by a hosting provider, and the typefaces are delivered by Google Fonts. Your browser contacts their servers to load the pages and fonts, which means they receive the standard technical details of a web request, such as your IP address. We do not receive that information, and none of your keys or progress is included in those requests.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct it if it is wrong;
  • Delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it;
  • Object to how we use it.

These include your rights under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To use any of them, contact us through the Contact page. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

How long we keep information

We keep order information only as long as we need it to support your purchase and to meet legal and accounting requirements, such as keeping tax records for the period the law requires. After that we delete it.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will also tell affected customers by email before it takes effect.

Questions

For any privacy question or request, contact us through the Contact page.
