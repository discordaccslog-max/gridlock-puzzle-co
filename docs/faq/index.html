FAQ

Questions and answers

How do license keys work?

Every purchase comes with a license key, a code that looks like GRID-DG-7KQ4M-X9D2P-H6TR. The two characters after GRID say which product it is for, and the last group is a check that catches typing mistakes. Your key appears on your order confirmation page the moment your payment goes through. Enter it once and your puzzle books open in your library. There is no account and no password.

Where do I enter my key?

On the Unlock page, which you can reach at any time from the Have a key? link at the top of every page. Type the characters after GRID, or paste the whole key into the first box. The confirmation page also has an Open my puzzles button that fills it in for you.

How should I save my key?

Copy it somewhere you keep important things: a password manager, a note on your phone, or the email you used to pay. The confirmation page has a Copy key button and a Save as a text file button. Your library page also lists the keys saved on that device.

Can I use more than one device?

Yes. The same key works on any number of your own computers, tablets and phones. On each new device, open the Unlock page and enter the key again.

What if I lose my key?

If the device you unlocked still shows your library, the key is listed there. Otherwise, contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we will send a replacement.

What is the difference between Core Books and Expert Editions?

The five Core Books cover the classics: sudoku, word search, cryptograms, logic grids and crosswords, each with a range from easy to expert. The Expert Editions are built to be harder in specific ways you can check: added sudoku rules such as cages and irregular regions, larger logic grids with more categories, full 15×15 crossword grids, fewer starting digits, and cryptograms with the word breaks removed. Each Expert Edition page lists exactly how it differs.

Can I print the puzzles?

Yes. Every puzzle has a Print button that produces a clean page with just the puzzle and its clues, and an Answer key button that prints the solution separately.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Progress is saved in the browser on each device, and it never leaves that device. A puzzle you start on your laptop will not appear half-finished on your phone. Each puzzle has a Reset progress button if you want to start it again.

Can teachers or puzzle clubs use these?

Each purchase is a personal license. If you would like to use the puzzles with a class or a club, contact us through the Contact page and tell us roughly how many people you have in mind; we offer group licenses.

What is your refund policy?

You can have a full refund on any purchase within 14 days, with no reason needed. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of your request. The full details are in the Refund Policy.
